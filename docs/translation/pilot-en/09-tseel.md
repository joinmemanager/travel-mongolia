Tseel soum is a farming soum that alone grows one third of the wheat harvest of Tuv Province (aimag). Many people still know it by the name of the former "Zaluuchuud" (Youth) state farm. Near the soum center, which sits in a hollow surrounded by mountains, are Burkhantai Lake and Khar Lake, rich in feather grass.

## How do I get to Tseel soum?

From Ulaanbaatar, drive 100 km west on a paved road to the settlement of Atar in [Bayankhangai soum](/province/tuv#bayankhangai) of Tuv Province. From there, take the dirt road branching northwest for 60 km, pass through [Ugtaaltsaidam](</province/tuv/ugtaaltsaidam>) soum, and drive another 20 km to reach Tseel soum. For other soums of the province, read the [Tuv Province page](/province/tuv).

## What is the history of the soum?

Tseel soum split from Ugtaaltsaidam soum in 1976 and took its present form. Households were first settled separately here to set up a new virgin-lands farm. In 1977 it officially became Tseel soum, adding one soum to Mongolia. The first head of the state farm was Begziin Batchuluun, Hero of Labor of Mongolia. He is said to have received the title Hero of Labor for supplying more than 550,000 tons of top-grade wheat to the state flour and fodder fund.

## Why is it called a farming soum?

The soum's native residents are farming people, and they can be divided into three groups: crop growers, vegetable growers and herders. Today many residents, businesses and companies grow crops in Tseel, so the soum produces one third of the province's wheat. In 2010 it led the province in its wheat harvest. It is also the soum that has produced the most National Champion Farmers. Besides crops, it is famous for its hayfields and pastures. Because it lies in a forest-steppe basin, its plants are rich and nourishing, and herders from many provinces, such as Uvs and Bayan-Ulgii, come here on otor (seasonal long-distance grazing).

## What are the nature and landscape like?

The soum lies in the forest-steppe zone of the Orkhon and Selenge basins. The west and north of the soum center, however, are sandy and look like the Gobi. Locals call this area "Elsen Tsagaan" (White Sands), and many Asian bird cherry trees grow there. The soum center sits in a small hollow surrounded by mountains. Behind it to the north stands Maikhan Mountain, and beyond it Shakhait Mountain closes off the northern edge of the soum. To the south rises Talyn Khairkhan, with small rocks and a steep, cliff-like slope, and to the west stand Ikh Khishigt and Baga Khishigt Khairkhan side by side.

## What are Burkhantai Lake and Khar Lake like?

Burkhantai Lake lies less than 10 km north of the soum center. It got its name from the statue of a Buddha beside it. In summer families camp around the lake and livestock gather there. On the eastern side of the lake is a small lake called Khar Lake, rich in feather grass. Its grassy valley stretches a long way, and it is said that even several thousand sheep disappear from view when they enter it.

## Frequently asked questions

### How far is Tseel soum from Ulaanbaatar?

It is 100 km on a paved road from Ulaanbaatar to the settlement of Atar, then 60 km on a dirt road to Ugtaaltsaidam, and another 20 km. That is about 180 km in total.

### What is Tseel soum known for?

It is known as the former "Zaluuchuud" state farm, as the soum that grows one third of Tuv Province's wheat, and as the soum that has produced the most National Champion Farmers.
