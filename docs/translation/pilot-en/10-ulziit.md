Ulziit soum of Uvurkhangai Province (aimag) is a steppe soum on the southern side of the eastern extension of the Khangai Range, about 2,000 meters above sea level. Once the banner (khoshuu) of Ilden Beil of Sain Noyon Khan aimag, this land became a soum in 1931.

## Where is Ulziit soum and how far is it?

The soum is in the northeast of Uvurkhangai Province. It is 375 km from Ulaanbaatar and 92 km from the provincial center. It borders [Yesunzuil](</province/uvurkhangai/yesunzuil>), Sant, Bayan-Undur, Zuunbayan-Ulaan and [Khujirt](/province/uvurkhangai/khujirt) soums. For other soums of the province, read the [Uvurkhangai Province page](/province/uvurkhangai).

## How many people live in Ulziit and what are its bags?

Ulziit soum has 4 bags: Borkhoshuu, Aguit, Yargait and Guulin. It has 2,472 people in 764 households. It ranks fifth from the bottom in the province by population and tenth by area.

## What mountains, plains and lakes are there?

The soum is ringed by mountains such as Khangi, Dulaan, and Baruun and Zuun Ulziit Khairkhan. It has low mountains, gorges and hills of the southern Khangai Range, and wide plains and valleys such as Tukhum, Joroo, Undur Ders, and Ekhen, Dund and Adgiin Tsagaan. There are many lakes and ponds that water people and livestock. They include Sangiin Dalai, Zegestei, Gorkhi, the Khuis River, Baruun and Zuun Tsegeen, the Northern, Southern and Middle Buur ponds, Murun, Ulaan Am, Ikh and Baga Bulag, the Yargait melt pond, Takhilgat and Asgat. Most of the land is steppe, with a small larch forest in the north. Brown soils and desert-steppe soils dominate, which suit farming and herding. Underground there are minerals such as mica, fluorite and hard coal.

## What are the climate and seasons like?

Ulziit has a harsh continental climate. The ground is stony, the weather is unsettled, and there are cold and cool spells, strong winds, and dust and snow storms from time to time.

- **Winter.** From October to April most of the land is covered with snow. On average 10–30 cm of snow falls, and temperatures drop to −10 to −28 °C. The average January temperature is −22 °C.
- **Spring and summer.** It warms up from May, and summers are 8–25 °C. The average July temperature is +19 °C.
- **Autumn.** It starts to get cold in early October.
- **Precipitation and wind.** Average annual precipitation is 200–260 mm. The average annual wind speed is 4–8 m/s.

The main plants are feather grass, needle grass and fescue.
