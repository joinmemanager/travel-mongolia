Bayan-Ovoo soum is a vast Gobi soum in Umnugovi Province (aimag) that shares a 75 km border with China. Here you will find long depressions grown with reeds, bamboo grass and tamarisk, medicinal plants, mineral resources, and monuments that preserve many centuries of history, from the Old Stone Age to the Qing era.

## Where is Bayan-Ovoo soum and how far is it?

The soum is 669 km from Ulaanbaatar and 165 km from Dalanzadgad, the provincial center. It is about 100 km from each of its neighbors, [Nomgon](/province/umnugov/nomgon), [Khanbogd](/province/umnugov/khanbogd) and Tsogttsetsii soums. The soum stretches 172 km long and 98 km wide, and to the south it shares a 75 km border with China. For other soums of the province, read the [Umnugovi Province page](/province/umnugov).

## How did the Govi Tusheet Gun banner become Bayan-Ovoo?

Long ago this land was called the banner (khoshuu) of Govi Tusheet Gun in Tusheet Khan aimag. On May 26, 1924, it was founded at a place called Erdenetolgoi under the name Shankhai Uul banner. The soum, which had 7–8 bags until 1958, was then settled at a place called Dolooshar Zag and named Bayan-Ovoo. In 1975 and 1980 the soum reached 10,000 camels and received the "Double Silver Hump" award.

## What mountains and valleys are there?

The soum has scenic mountains that preserve ancient historical and cultural monuments, such as Zakh Gurvan Zeemeg, the great Zag Suuj Gobi, Ikh and Baga Khachig, Undur Bogd, Argalant, Togoo, Jargalant, Khuut, Tsagaan Del, Shivee and Yamaat Ulaan. Between them lie wide plains and valleys such as the Gederegiin Khets, Bunkhant Plain and the Zeergenet Valley. From the Borzon Gobi to the Galbyn Gobi stretches a long, wide depression of 3,500 km² grown with reeds, bamboo grass and tamarisk. It is called the Gurgem, Daichin or Zag Suuj Gobi, and it covers the entire southwestern part of the soum.

## What are the water, plants and climate like?

Places such as Den Tsagaan Khoshuu, Sharzag, the Guramsan Valley and Tavan Ald have good reserves of clean groundwater. More than 500 kinds of plants grow in the soum, and about 40 of them are medicinal. A special feature is the wild black millet that grows in a small part of the Zeemeg Gobi. The climate is harsh but rather warm. Winters have little snow, summers have rare rainfall, and springs are very stormy. Average annual precipitation is 63–70 mm.

## What mineral resources are there?

There is the Botgon silver deposit, the Alag Tolgoi gold reserve and the Yamaat Mountain copper deposit. There are also rich reserves of coal, oil shale, fluorite, limestone and building stones such as marble. The oil shale here formed in the Cretaceous period, 120–160 million years ago, and produces heat of 2,000 calories.

## What historical monuments are in Bayan-Ovoo soum?

**Traces of ancient people.** Traces of a camp that prove ancient humans lived in Asia were found on Otson Maant Mountain. They are dated to the end of the Old Stone Age, 600,000–300,000 years ago. **Ruins of towns and forts.** At Sairyn Balgas in Nalikh bag there are the foundations of a walled Xiongnu (Hunnu) town and the remains of a Qing-era fort and trading town. There are also ruins of an ancient town on the road from Zag Suuj to Ikh Alag Mountain. **The Yellow Road.** The great relay-station and trade road to Khovd and Uliastai, known as the "Yellow Road", once crossed this soum. Coming up from Beijing and the Zhangjiakou pass, it reached the soum at the Gedereg Pass. **Monuments of the great khans.** There are historic sites such as Duut Rock, Chinggis's bow-and-quiver stand on the Argalant ridge, the hollows where the great khans tied their horses in the Guramsan depression, Khulgar, the birthplace of Batmunkh Dayan Khan, the Muruin Gorge, and the window paintings at the Military Ruins of the Great Khans. **Rare natural finds.** This vast Gobi holds valuable historical and cultural finds such as the Nalikh petrified wood, a 4 m petrified snake, the Khurkh stone figure and a six-sided stone.

## What services are in the soum center?

The soum center has a general education school for 280 children, a kindergarten for 50 children, a 15-bed hospital, a post and communications office and a weather station.

## Frequently asked questions

### How far is Bayan-Ovoo soum from Dalanzadgad?

It is 165 km from Dalanzadgad, the provincial center, and 669 km from Ulaanbaatar. Nomgon, Khanbogd and Tsogttsetsii soums are each about 100 km away.

### What historical places can I see in Bayan-Ovoo soum?

There is Khulgar, the birthplace of Batmunkh Dayan Khan, Chinggis's bow-and-quiver stand, the foundations of a walled Xiongnu town at Sairyn Balgas, and the Nalikh petrified wood.
