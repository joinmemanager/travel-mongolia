It reads: "By the power of the Eternal Sky, under the khan's blessing, recalling an event, written on the wall as a monument, as a symbol, on the eighth day of the new moon of the ninth month of the Year of the Rooster."

## What does the square script inscription look like?

Next to the Mongolian inscription, there is a monument in square script with seven lines. Over the years it has worn away badly and eroded, so its meaning has not been fully made out.

## What paintings are on the rocks?

Besides the inscriptions, the following figures were painted in ocher on the rocks:

- a flying bird;
- a human figure inside a square frame;
- many dots that may be a count of livestock;
- short-tailed animals, deer, and animals resembling a roe deer;
- vertical lines;
- a naked male figure with arms spread and legs apart.

## What is Kheree Mountain?

- **Location:** Khalkhgol soum, Dornod Province, about 60 km southeast of the soum center, on the west bank of the Khalkh River.
- **Archaeology:** The Kheree Mountain site, from the Middle Stone Age.
- **Excavation:** 1967.

## What is the Kheree Mountain site?

In archaeology, the finds from the Middle Stone Age are known as the "Kheree Mountain site." They were excavated in 1967. Of the 4 soil layers, 3 belong to the Stone Age cultural layer. The site is special because it is the first Middle Stone Age settlement ever found in Mongolia.

## What was found during the excavation?

- cores made of flint;
- rectangular scrapers made of flakes, with a convex edge;
- black shale knives, or blades;
- large and small triangular blades, both retouched and unretouched;
- items resembling chipped fragments.

## What is on the summit of Kheree Mountain?

On the summit, people built 13 stone ovoos and hold worship ceremonies there, matching the number of shamanic shrines in Mongolian shamanism. From the top, you can see the eastern border strip and the scenic nature of the Khalkh [Numrug](</destination/protected/numrug>) strictly protected area.
