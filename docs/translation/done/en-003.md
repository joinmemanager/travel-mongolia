## How did Shiveegov begin?

Under Qing rule and during the Bogd Khan era of Mongolia, this land was the core territory of the banner (khoshuu) of Borjigin Setsen Wang in the Setsen Khan Province of the Khalkha. ## What makes Shiveegov different?

Shiveegov is different from an ordinary rural sum. Most of its people live in the mining settlement and in apartments, and daily life is closely tied to the coal mine and the railway.
