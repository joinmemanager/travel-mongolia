## What are the Badrakh Uvur deer stones?

- **Location:** Khuvsgul Province, Arbulag soum, Badrakh Uvur.
- **Stone enclosure:** 7x7 m.
- **Number:** three deer stones, facing south.
- **Stone no. 2:** 1.05 m tall, 30 cm wide.
- **Carvings:** deer, sun, belt, ax, knife, shield.

## What is carved on deer stone no. 2?

Of the three stones, stone no. 2 is the clearest example:

- one deer on the wide side, shown leaping upward;
- a belt at the bottom, with an ax and a knife hanging from it;
- round shapes struck in a row along the upper edge;
- a sun at the top of the wide side.

## What is on the narrow sides of the stone?

- On one narrow side, a deer and two crossing lines.
- On the other narrow side, deer antlers and a shield.

The Khavtsalyn Amny deer stone is an ancient monument in a small gorge mouth called Khavtsalyn. It lies in Ikhtamir soum, [Arkhangai Province (aimag)](/province/arkhangai), below the center of Bugat bag (the smallest administrative unit). The gorge opens into the Bayantsagaan River valley from the west.

## What are the key facts about the Khavtsalyn Amny deer stone?

- **Location:** Arkhangai Province, Ikhtamir soum, Bugat bag, Bayantsagaan River valley.
- **Height:** 2.2 m.
- **Width:** 0.52 m.
- **Thickness:** 0.44 m.
- **Carvings:** two suns, ten deer, a belt, a dagger, a shield.

## What is carved on the upper part of the stone?

- One ring, or sun, at the top of both the back and the front.
- Rays like a stalk below the sun on the front.
- Below that, a row of many small hollows running around the stone.

## How are the deer carved?

In the center of the stone, ten deer are carved in a stylized way. Their heads point toward the top of the stone.

## How are the belt and weapons carved?

Below the deer, a belt is carved with a pattern like saw blades. A dagger hangs from the belt. On the back of the stone, a shield is carved beside the belt.

## Where is Sumiin Khad? Quick facts

- **Location:** Khentii Province, 23 km east of Galshar soum center.
- **Formation:** large rocks and scree that look layered.
- **Notable spots:** Khatan Suudal, Camel Rock, Ger Rock.
- **Wildlife:** argali, khulan.

## How big is Sumiin Khad?

It covers a much smaller area than [Ikh Gazryn Chuluu](</destination/protected/ikh-gazryn-chuluu>) and [Baga Gazryn Chuluu](</destination/landscapes/baga-gazryn-chuluu>). Even so, it is fairly large to walk around. Local people did not know its exact size.

## What is there to see at Sumiin Khad?

- Rocks with unusual names, such as Khatan Suudal, Camel Rock and Ger Rock;
- Large rock formations that look layered;
- Argali and khulan running across the land.

## When is the best time to visit Sumiin Khad?

Domestic travelers come to Galshar soum for the [Galshar Horse Festival](</about/traditions/galsharyn-aduu>) and the "Mongol Khuu" Naadam (festival) for child riders. It is a good time to visit Sumiin Khad as well.
