## How did printing begin in Mongolia?

## How was the building constructed?

- **1923.** The government instructed Erdenebatkhaan, the Minister of People's Education, to set up a printing house. It approved spending 30,000 lan of silver to build a single-story house 24 paces long and 12 paces wide.

The broken summit landed behind the mountain. Locals avoid saying its name nearby. It is called Tovkh Mountain.
